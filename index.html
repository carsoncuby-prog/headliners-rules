NO PURCHASE, GIFT, TIPPING, PAID SUBSCRIPTION, OR DONATION NECESSARY. Sending gifts or spending money does not earn points, affect placement, or improve the chance of winning. Participation requires only free TikTok LIVE and Discord activities subject to ordinary internet access costs.

5. HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Join The Madhouse Discord, register your TikTok username in #headliners-club, and press the LIVE check-in button for each qualifying stream. Check-in alone does not earn points. A code entered on Discord is only one signal of participation; organizers may verify account association and activity. Codes are announced during the stream and remain open for approximately five minutes each. Staff verify legitimate chat engagement and game participation; unsolicited spam, mass comments, or disruptive behavior do not qualify.

6. SCORING PER QUALIFYING LIVE (MAX 75)

First correctly submitted code: 10 points.
Second correctly submitted code: 15 points.
Verified meaningful TikTok LIVE chat participation: 25 points.
Verified LIVE game or series participation: 15 points.
Completion bonus for both correct codes and verified chat engagement: 10 points.
A member can receive each award at most once per qualifying LIVE. Activity must occur during that LIVE and be approved or recorded before weekly results are finalized. Code sharing, false check-ins, automated activity, impersonation, multi-accounting, and other manipulation may lead to rejected activity or disqualification. Score corrections are documented by staff.

7. CHAMPION SELECTION AND TIES

The eligible participant with the highest verified total at the weekly cutoff is the provisional champion. Ties are resolved by (1) most fully verified qualifying LIVEs, then (2) most successful code checkpoints, then (3) an organizer-administered free, objective tie-break challenge announced equally to tied participants and documented before finalization. Organizers do not award a cash prize by an unexplained random tie-break. A provisional result may be adjusted to correct documented errors and enforce the rules.

A gift card alternative may be offered only if its brand/type and equivalence are disclosed in the pre-week terms and accepted by the winner; otherwise the announced prize terms control. No substitute of lower value. Award availability remains subject to applicable platform terms and law.
